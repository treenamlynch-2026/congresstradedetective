Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

What this site collects

This website does not use accounts, advertising trackers, or analytics cookies. If you use the contact form, the name, email address, and message you enter are sent to us through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service, so we can reply. We use that information only to respond to you.

The tracker and apps

Your watchlist is stored on your own device. If you turn on push alerts in the mobile app, a device token and the tickers you watch are sent to our server so alerts can be delivered. You can turn alerts off at any time in your device settings.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Those providers may receive your IP address as part of normal web requests. Links to YouTube and official government sites are governed by those sites' own policies.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
